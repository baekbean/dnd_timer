# Launch copy

Reference copy for outbound launch posts, from the SEO/GEO audit. **Nothing here is
posted automatically** — each item is a manual post, in the priority order below
(easiest → highest leverage).

Positioning to keep consistent across all of these: no account, no gamification of
any kind (not even optional), and the tab-title countdown. Those are the three
claims competitors can't make, and they're the ones the site copy and the
`WebApplication` JSON-LD now lead with. See `lib/seo.ts`.

---

## Product Hunt

**Tagline** (60 char max):

> A calm focus timer that fits into your workspace, not your face

**First comment / description:**

> Hey PH — built NookTimer because most Pomodoro timers either look like a
> stopwatch bolted onto a to-do app, or bury the timer under gamification
> (streaks, leaderboards, badges). NookTimer is the opposite: pick a calming
> scene (meadow, dusk, night), toggle ambient sound, run a Pomodoro cycle, and
> the countdown just lives quietly in your tab title while you work. No signup,
> no streaks to keep alive, nothing to lose if you skip a day. Would love
> feedback on the scenes/sound pairing especially.

## Reddit

Targets: r/PomodoroTechnique, r/productivity, r/GetStudying

**Title:**

> Made a free Pomodoro timer with ambient scenes instead of streaks/gamification — feedback welcome

**Body:**

> Got tired of focus timers that guilt-trip you over broken streaks, so I built
> one that's just... quiet. Pick a scene (meadow / dusk / night), optional
> ambient sound, standard 25/5 or custom Pomodoro cycle. Runs in-browser, no
> account. [nooktimer.com] Genuinely looking for feedback on whether the scenes
> actually help focus or if they're just decoration — curious what this sub
> thinks.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Check each subreddit's self-promotion rules before posting. Several require a
> minimum comment history, and some only allow self-promo in a designated
> "Show and Tell" thread on a specific day of the week. Posting cold into these
> subs without checking is the fastest way to get the domain shadowbanned.

Cold outreach asking to be added to "best free Pomodoro timer" roundups. Sites
found ranking for that query at audit time:

- focustimer.online
- studiestimer.com
- pomodorotimer.online

Lead with the differentiator rather than a generic "please add us" — the
no-gamification angle is what makes NookTimer a distinct entry in a list rather
than a fourth near-duplicate.
