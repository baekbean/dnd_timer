# Launch copy

Reference copy for outbound launch posts, from the SEO/GEO audit. **Nothing here is
posted automatically** — each item is a manual post, in the priority order below
(easiest → highest leverage).

Positioning to keep consistent across all of these: no gamification of any kind
(not even optional), the tab-title countdown, and the YouTube-video background
option. Those are the claims competitors can't make. ("No account" is still true
and still worth a mention, but the 2026-08-20 GEO audit found it's now standard
copy across most of the free Pomodoro-timer field — pomotimer.com, pomofy.com,
pomodotree.com, pomodoronline.com all lead with it too — so don't lean on it as
the differentiator anymore.) See `lib/seo.ts`.

## Brand consistency

Use **NookTimer** as the product and organization name on every external profile,
directory listing, byline, and link description. Keep the canonical URL as
`https://nooktimer.com` and reuse the same core product description so search
engines and AI systems receive one consistent entity signal.

## AlternativeTo

Submit as a new item at alternativeto.net if `nooktimer` isn't already listed.

**Short description** (shown in search results and listing cards, keep near 1
sentence — deliberately reordered from the `lib/seo.ts` site description below
so the differentiator leads, not "no account"):

> A free online focus timer with no gamification, ever — calming scenes,
> ambient sounds, and a countdown that follows you tab to tab.

**Tag as "alternative to":** Pomofocus, Flocus, Forest, TomatoTimer — tagging
existing apps is what surfaces NookTimer on their "Alternatives" pages, which
is the actual traffic/citation source, not the NookTimer page itself.

**Platform:** Web · **License:** Free · **Category:** Time Tracking / Office &
Productivity

**Longer description** (optional field — use it, don't leave blank):

> NookTimer is a Pomodoro-style focus timer built around one idea: no
> gamification, ever. No streaks, no badges, no account. Pick a calming scene
> (meadow, dusk, night) or drop in a YouTube video as your own background,
> layer on ambient sound, and run a standard or custom focus cycle. The
> countdown stays visible in your browser tab title, so you can check it
> without switching back. Free, runs entirely in-browser.

> [!NOTE]
> Don't lean on "no account" as the headline here — visitors are comparing
> feature-by-feature against Forest and Flocus, both of which also require no
> account for the free tier. No-gamification and the tab-title countdown are
> the claims they can't match.
